## About This Process

Each complaint folder contains:

- **Case studies** — representative domain breakdowns with live screenshots

All correspondence is published under our transparency policy.  
Materials may not be used by citizens of the Russian Federation, Belarus, or OFAC-sanctioned jurisdictions.
